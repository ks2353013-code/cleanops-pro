# CleanOps Pro — Fast Go-Live Plan

## Launch mode

CleanOps should launch as an operations-first B2B cleaning platform. Avoid blocking the first customers on every advanced integration. The initial operating release needs reliable customer intake, facility records, assessment/quoting, contracts, scheduling, dispatch, professional execution, QA, invoicing and issue handling.

## Day-one workflows

### Customer
1. Register organization.
2. Add facility.
3. Submit cleaning requirement.
4. Receive assessment/quote.
5. Accept commercial terms.
6. View scheduled jobs.
7. Review completion/evidence.
8. Raise issue or approve service.
9. View invoices and outstanding balance.

### Operations
1. View new requests.
2. Assess scope and risk.
3. Produce quote.
4. Convert approved quote to contract.
5. Generate recurring work.
6. Assign qualified professionals.
7. Monitor check-ins and exceptions.
8. Inspect completed work.
9. Trigger rework/corrective action when needed.
10. Generate/track invoices.

### Professional
1. Maintain profile and availability.
2. View eligible assignments.
3. Accept/acknowledge assignment.
4. Check in.
5. Execute SOP checklist.
6. Capture required evidence.
7. Report incident/scope change.
8. Check out.
9. View completed-work history/earnings when enabled.

## Fastest safe deployment sequence

1. Production database and migrations.
2. Authentication and tenant authorization verification.
3. Client intake and facility workflow.
4. Operations dispatch workflow.
5. Professional mobile-responsive workflow.
6. Billing records and invoice export/payment integration.
7. Email notifications.
8. Object storage for evidence/documents.
9. Monitoring, backups and error reporting.
10. End-to-end smoke tests for school, hospital and office scenarios.

## Explicit non-blocking enhancements

Advanced AI optimization, native mobile packaging, payroll-provider integrations, advanced accounting integrations, sophisticated route optimization and marketplace-style worker discovery can follow the first operational release. They should be architected for, but should not delay a reliable core service-delivery workflow.

## Go-live gate

Do not market the platform as fully automated until real authenticated database-backed workflows have been executed successfully in staging. Every customer-facing action must have an authorized API path and persistent record. Every operational mutation must be traceable to an actor and organization.
