# CleanOps Pro — Operations SOP

## Service request
1. Capture customer, facility, requested service, area, frequency, preferred window and urgency.
2. For high-risk facilities, capture access, PPE, restricted-zone and customer-specific requirements.
3. Assign an assessment before issuing a non-standard commercial quote.

## Site assessment
1. Validate facility type and usable area.
2. Record risk classification and service scope.
3. Estimate labor hours and recommended staffing.
4. Record assumptions so quote revisions are explainable.

## Quote and contract
1. Generate price from approved cost assumptions.
2. Apply target margin and taxes as configured.
3. Record validity, inclusions and exclusions.
4. Require authorized customer acceptance before recurring service begins.
5. Create SLA terms and escalation contacts.

## Dispatch
1. Match only active, available and appropriately classified professionals.
2. Prefer qualified workers with good performance and practical travel distance.
3. Confirm shift, site access and PPE requirements.
4. If a professional misses the check-in threshold, trigger supervisor attention and replacement search.

## Execution
1. Professional checks in.
2. Complete facility/service-specific SOP checklist.
3. Capture required evidence where contract policy requires it.
4. Report incidents, access problems, damage or scope changes immediately.
5. Check out only after the assigned work is complete or explicitly escalated.

## Quality
1. Supervisor/customer inspection where configured.
2. Calculate quality score and SLA status.
3. Failed work creates a corrective action with owner and deadline.
4. Rework is tracked as a distinct operational event, not silently overwritten.

## Billing
1. Bill only approved contract terms and billable completed work.
2. Keep invoice status separate from service status.
3. Track payment date, outstanding balance and aging bucket.
4. Escalate overdue receivables according to customer policy.

## Safety and compliance
CleanOps should record compliance evidence and enforce customer/site policies. It must not represent a worker as certified unless the relevant record has been verified. Regulatory requirements vary by jurisdiction and facility; configurable policy is preferred over hard-coded legal assumptions.

## Incident principle
Safety incidents, data-security incidents and financial discrepancies are never hidden by editing the original operational record. Preserve the history, record the correction and escalate to the appropriate authorized role.
